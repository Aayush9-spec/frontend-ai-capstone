> *Why It Was Killed:* While visually flashy, it failed the identity kit's mood of "minimal high-precision technical clarity." > **Rejected Image #2 (Architecture Diagram Graphic):**  
> *Generated Prompt:* "3D isometric glass cube representing database microservices with glowing blue arrows."  
> *Why It Was Killed:* The 3D isometric rendering introduced inconsistent lighting and shadow angles that conflicted with our 2D flat geometric UI kit (`#0F172A` / `#F8FAFC`). Replaced with a clean, flat Mermaid SVG diagram matching exact palette hex codes (`#2563EB` accent).
